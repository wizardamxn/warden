# Notes screens

Paste a link, watch the job progress, read the notes next to the video with clickable timestamps.

- Job progress: a plain progress line or simple spinner showing the real job step.
- Live section highlight: a highlighter stroke sweeps under the heading of the section being played, and scrolls it into view.
- Focus mode: one key narrows the light; only the video and the playing section stay lit.
- Job progress glow: the sunny glow behind the page grows as each step finishes.
- Video can't be embedded: notes on their own, timestamps open youtube.com in a new tab.
