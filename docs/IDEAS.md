# Ideas

Aman's ideas, not decided yet. Moved here from README.md on 2026-11-08. Each has Claude's notes from the discussion under it.

## Past NEET questions in the quizzes

> Something like we can grab all the past questions from NEET, and then when we watch those lectures or paste those notes, the AI grabs the relevant questions and adds them in the quiz/tests/etc.? An agent!? Will think later.

- The strongest idea: real exam questions on what was just watched.
- It would also fix exam marks: "Asked 2024" would come from the question bank, not from Gemini reading the teacher's words.
- Doesn't need an agent: tag each past question with its NCERT chapter and topic once, then pick matching questions after each lecture. A lookup and a ranking, as a fixed step.
- The hard part is the data: collecting past papers with correct answers, tagging them, and checking we're allowed to reuse them.

## A public library of notes

> Maybe we can create a public repository or library so people can access notes without pasting links?

- Mostly built already: finished notes are reused by video ID, so this is a page and a search, with no extra AI cost.
- Risks: a public index of notes from coaching lectures is more visible, and could draw complaints. The plan also says no growth work until the MVP is with students.

## Beyond NEET

Decided 2026-11-08: Warden is a universal study helper, for anyone studying from YouTube lectures. See README.md and docs/PLAN.md. The notes below are from before that decision.

> We don't want to focus on NEET only, but JEE and other government exams, and coders as well?

- The engine already allows it: only the prompts and quiz style are NEET-specific.
- The plan keeps other exams out of scope until NEET students are using it.
- Coders would be a different product: the notes, the tests and what "done" means all change.
